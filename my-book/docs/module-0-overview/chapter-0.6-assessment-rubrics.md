# Chapter 0.6: Assessment & Rubrics

This chapter outlines the assessment strategy and rubrics for the "Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics" course. Our goal is to evaluate your understanding of key concepts, your ability to apply theoretical knowledge to practical problems, and your proficiency in utilizing the course's recommended software and hardware tools. Assessment is designed to be progressive, with each component reinforcing your learning journey.

## 1. Assessment Components

Your overall grade in this course will be determined by a combination of the following components:

-   **Module Assignments (40% of final grade)**: Each of Modules 1-4 will include a hands-on assignment or mini-project. These are designed to test your understanding of the module's core concepts and your ability to implement practical solutions.
-   **Capstone Project (40% of final grade)**: The culminating project of the course, requiring integration of knowledge from all modules. This is a significant undertaking that demonstrates your comprehensive understanding and practical skills. Refer to Chapter 0.5 for detailed Capstone Design Specifications.
-   **Participation & Engagement (10% of final grade)**: Active participation in discussions, constructive feedback on peer work (if applicable), and consistent engagement with course materials.
-   **Quizzes / Short Challenges (10% of final grade)**: Short, low-stakes quizzes or coding challenges designed to reinforce key theoretical concepts and ensure foundational understanding.

## 2. Grading Scale

The following grading scale will be used:

| Percentage | Grade |
| :--------- | :---- |
| 90-100%    | A     |
| 80-89%     | B     |
| 70-79%     | C     |
| 60-69%     | D     |
| Below 60%  | F     |

## 3. Rubrics

Each major assignment and the Capstone Project will be assessed using specific rubrics. These rubrics provide clear criteria for evaluation, allowing you to understand expectations and guide your work. General rubric categories include:

### 3.1. Module Assignment Rubric (Example Categories)

| Category                | 4 - Excellent                                    | 3 - Good                                         | 2 - Fair                                           | 1 - Needs Improvement                              |
| :---------------------- | :----------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **Functionality**       | All requirements met; solution works flawlessly. | Most requirements met; minor issues present.     | Some requirements met; significant issues.         | Few requirements met; largely non-functional.      |
| **Code Quality**        | Clean, well-documented, efficient, Pythonic.     | Readable, some documentation, generally efficient. | Messy, sparse documentation, inefficiencies.       | Unreadable, no documentation, major inefficiencies.|
| **Conceptual Understanding** | Demonstrates deep understanding of concepts.     | Good understanding, minor conceptual gaps.         | Basic understanding, several conceptual errors.    | Lacks basic understanding.                         |
| **Problem Solving**     | Innovative, effective solutions to challenges.   | Effective solutions, some guidance needed.         | Struggles with problem-solving, much guidance needed.| Unable to solve problems.                          |
| **ROS 2 / Tool Usage**  | Proficient and idiomatic use of tools.           | Adequate use of tools.                           | Basic use of tools, some errors.                   | Incorrect or no use of tools.                      |

### 3.2. Capstone Project Rubric (Key Categories from Chapter 0.5)

The Capstone Project rubric will expand upon the Module Assignment rubric, with particular emphasis on:

-   **Functionality**: How well the humanoid robot performs the intended multi-step task based on natural language commands.
-   **Technical Depth**: Quality and sophistication of the ROS 2 integration, simulation (Gazebo/Isaac Sim), AI perception (Isaac ROS), and VLA system.
-   **Innovation/Creativity**: Originality in task definition, problem-solving approaches, or extensions.
-   **Code Quality**: Adherence to Pythonic standards, modularity, readability, and maintainability.
-   **Documentation**: Clarity, completeness, and organization of the technical report, code comments, and project README.
-   **Demonstration & Presentation**: Effectiveness in showcasing the project, explaining design choices, and articulating technical challenges and solutions.

Detailed rubrics for each assignment and the Capstone Project will be provided at the beginning of the relevant module. We encourage you to review these rubrics carefully to guide your work and maximize your learning.
